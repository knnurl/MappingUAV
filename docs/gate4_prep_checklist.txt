# filepath: docs/gate4_prep_checklist.txt
Preconditions, all verified and logged before first tethered spin-up:

[ ] Gates 1-3 formally passed and their transcripts stored in docs/.
[ ] gate3_ekf2.params re-read back from the FC and diffed against the file
    (params can silently fail to save before a reboot).
[ ] Mid-360 on foam standoffs; wiring strain-relieved; nothing enters the
    lidar's blind radius when arms flex.
[ ] Props balanced and correct rotation; motors mapped (Actuators test in QGC, props OFF first).
[ ] Kill switch assigned and tested: RC kill -> immediate motor stop, while disarmed-bench-spinning.
[ ] Vibration re-check WITH props at hover throttle on the tether:
    fly nothing, just spin at low throttle strapped down, pull the log,
    Flight Review vibration metrics in the green band. If not, LIO health under
    vibration must be re-verified (repeat Gate 2 checks with motors running).
[ ] Flow/range sanity: listener distance_sensor and listener optical_flow (or
    sensor_optical_flow, [VERIFY] per firmware) show live plausible data.
[ ] Tether anchored below and clear of props; test volume >= 3 x 3 x 2.5 m clear.
[ ] Flight mode POSITION, MPC limits confirmed active (attempt aggressive stick
    input on the bench, observe setpoint clamps in the log).
[ ] Recording policy in force: rosbag limited to /Odometry and
    /fmu/out/vehicle_odometry; PX4 .ulg logging enabled (that is the primary record).

Gate 4 pass criteria (human pilot in the loop, from the master plan):
position hold within +/- 10 cm for 2 continuous minutes, zero EKF resets,
no LIO degradation events in the log.
