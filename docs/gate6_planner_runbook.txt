LIMITS OF THIS PLANNER (know them before flying it)
  L1  Static scenes only. It re-checks the path once per second against a map
      updated twice per second. Nothing reacts faster. NOBODY walks into the
      volume during a run.
  L2  It flies only through cells the LiDAR has SEEN free, with 0.4 m
      clearance. A goal in unseen space is refused ("goal FAILED: goal cell is
      not observed free"). That is correct behaviour.
  L3  Glass is invisible to the LiDAR. Keep glazing outside the geofence.
  L4  After ANY Offboard exit the platform forwards nothing until T3 is
      restarted; the planner then refuses goals ("not in Offboard").
  L5  Path tracking error is unmeasured (D2). The clearance is 0.4 m from the
      vehicle CENTRE on a 0.2 m grid: real margin to a wall can be ~0.3 m.

STOP CONDITIONS: all of Gate 5's, plus
  - the vehicle passes closer to an obstacle than planned, or cuts a corner;
  - "path blocked" with nobody in the volume (map or drift problem);
  - /go_to_with_avoidance/status FAILED with a reason not caused on purpose.
  Pilot action is unchanged: flip to POSITION; if in doubt, KILL.

PART D: PROPS ON, TETHERED (tether long enough for the D1 goal)
==========================================================================
BEFORE EVERY FLIGHT: Gate 5 "before every flight" list; T3 and T5 freshly
restarted; preflight_check --as2 --mapping all PASS; logging running.
Take off with the Gate 5 D1 action to 1.3 m. Wait 10 s (map fills).

D1  One goal in free space, 2 m ahead (the C2 command).
    EXPECT status PLANNING -> EXECUTING -> REACHED; the vehicle flies FORWARD
    about 2 m at ~0.5 m/s and stops; heading unchanged (KEEP_YAW; closes
    OI-5). Any other direction: pilot takes over, STOP (frame error).
D2  Tracking: goal back to x 0. From the bag afterwards: largest sideways
    distance between /drone0/self_localization/pose and the straight line.
    Record it. Above 0.20 m: do not go to D4; tune first.
D3  Cancel and hold: send the D1 goal; when the vehicle is about half way:
      ros2 topic pub --once /geofence/hover_request std_msgs/msg/Empty
    EXPECT alert "STOPPED: hover requested ...", the vehicle stops within
    ~0.5 m and HOLDS for 30 s (drift under 0.15 m), PX4 still in Offboard.
    If it does not hold: pilot takes over, STOP. The planner then needs
    another stop mechanism before any further gate (closes OI-4).
D4  Obstacle: one box on the straight line between the mark and a goal 3 m
    ahead, placed BEFORE take-off so the map has it. Send the goal.
    EXPECT a path round the box with visible clearance on the chosen side.
D5  Unseen goal: a goal behind the box's shadow or beyond a closed door.
    EXPECT "goal FAILED: ... not observed free" or "no path", no motion.
D6  Takeover: during a D1 flight the pilot flips to POSITION.
    EXPECT the vehicle holds in POSITION, T3 logs "AS2 commands disabled",
    planner status FAILED "platform is not in Offboard". Land. Restart T3.
D7  LIO health: only if D1-D6 were clean. With the vehicle hovering after a
    REACHED, a helper walks briskly across the room 3 m away.
    Record /lio_health/state and the bridge status. No pass criterion: this
    measures how the estimate reacts to a person (Gate 2 saw decimetres).
Land with the Gate 5 land action after each flight.
