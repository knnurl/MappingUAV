# Written 2026-11-16 against lio-swap-prep and fork branch b1-health (1abee84).

WHAT THE SWAP IS NOW
  One launch argument. Every launch file takes lio:=upstream|fork and loads
  config/lio_<name>.yaml, the only place the LIO topics are named:
              odometry              world cloud              health
    upstream  /Odometry             /cloud_registered        (none; derived)
    fork      /fastlio2/lio_odom    /fastlio2/world_cloud    /fastlio2/lio_health
  Default everywhere: upstream. Nothing changes until lio:=fork is passed.

WHY IT NEEDS HARDWARE
  Gates 2 and 3 were passed on upstream. The fork has different filter code.
  Operator decision 2026-10-26: upstream flies Gate 4; the fork swaps in
  afterwards and the Gate 2 and Gate 3 checks are re-run on it.

WHAT WAS CHECKED WITHOUT HARDWARE (2026-11-16)
  - Fork replayed on three recordings: all scans valid, no odometry jumps
    (fork docs/lio_health.md).
  - Closed-loop rig with the fork's topic names and health message: full
    exploration mission, LIO jump lands, degraded native health stops the
    planner and the vehicle holds. The rig's fake LiDAR imitates the fork's
    interface; the fork's filter itself was NOT in that loop.
  - The fork's real lio_node replayed on the hallway recording into the real

PART A: GATE 2 CHECKS ON THE FORK (props OFF, bench, nobody within 3 m)
  ros2 launch drone_bringup gate2_lio.launch.py lio:=fork
A1 Rates, 15 s each:  /livox/lidar 10 Hz, /livox/imu ~200 Hz,
     /fastlio2/lio_odom ~10 Hz, /fastlio2/lio_health ~10 Hz.
     (The fork publishes odometry only while something subscribes.)
A2 Stationary 5 min after a 60 s settle: position noise and z drift.
     Upstream reference: median 1.1 cm, max 2.6 cm, z drift ~0.001 cm/min.
A3 Health while stationary:
     ros2 topic echo /fastlio2/lio_health --field translation_degeneracy
     Record min / median of translation_degeneracy, match_ratio,
     residual_rms. EXPECT valid true throughout; values inside the envelope
     in fork docs/lio_health.md (degeneracy >= 0.18, ratio >= 0.41,
     residual <= 6.6 cm). Outside it: stop and report; the guard's limits
     (0.08 / 0.25 / 10 cm) are provisional.
A4 Carry a closed loop back to the mark. Return-to-start gap; compare with
     upstream on the same loop the same day (the 2026-10-23 loop5 recording
     gave the fork 0.4-3.5 cm less gap than upstream on four of five runs).
A5 Compute: top for lio_node and the livox driver. Upstream reference:
     FAST-LIO 28 % of a core, 174 MB total. Now WITH the world cloud:
     start `ros2 topic hz /fastlio2/world_cloud` in another terminal and
     record lio_node CPU again.

PART B: GATE 3 CHECKS ON THE FORK (props OFF, FC powered)
  ros2 launch drone_bringup gate3_fusion.launch.py lio:=fork serial_dev:=<FTDI>
B1 bash scripts/verify_gate3.sh ; bridge log "Bridging /fastlio2/lio_odom".
     Status FORWARDING, rejected=0, and NO "Dropping LIO odometry: stamp age"
     warnings (the fork stamps with the scan end time; if these appear the
     stamp convention differs: STOP).
B2 Estimator flags as in the Gate 3 transcript section 2 (cs_ev_pos,
     cs_ev_yaw, cs_ev_hgt true).
B3 Sign and axis test by hand: forward, left, up, 90 deg clockwise yaw
     (transcript sections 4, 5, 14). A frame error hides here.
B4 PX4 estimate against LIO, still and moving, as in transcript section 14.
     Upstream reference with flow off: still 1-2 cm; moving median 4.7 cm,
     max 11.7 cm; zero resets.
B5 15 min untouched: reset counters unchanged, status FORWARDING.
B6 LiDAR power cut: guard STALE then BLOCKED (VD-011 behaviour holds).
B7 Health guard: ros2 launch ... exploration.launch.py lio:=fork explorer:=false
     guard log "native health from /fastlio2/lio_health"; /lio_health/state 0.
     Cover the LiDAR with a box for 3 s: record what the health values and
     /lio_health/state do (first look at a degraded reading on hardware).
