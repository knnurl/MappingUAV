  Discharged by: C1 (topic present) and C3 (observed truth table).

KNOWN HAZARDS AND DEFECTS (read from source 2026-09-13/14; not VD entries)

K1  RESOLVED by the offboard hold patch (K8): thrust before takeoff.
    Unpatched, while armed and before the controller sets a control mode,
    the platform streams a body-rate setpoint with thrust -min_thrust as a
    normalised 0..1 value (pixhawk_platform.cpp:254-262 at 482563b): about
    50% thrust on the ground with the old min_thrust 0.5, and no attitude
    control. The patch removes that stream. min_thrust is 0.15 (operator
    approved 2026-09-13) and only the unused ATTITUDE and ACRO paths read
    it now. C4 confirms on the bench.

K2  Offboard exit, disarm and restart (patched behaviour).
    Unpatched, after any Offboard exit the platform kept sending the last AS2
    velocity command, which PX4 mixed into POSITION or Land (K8). Patched:
    on any exit (pilot switch, watchdog NAV_LAND, failsafe) or disarm,
    setpoints stop at once and this platform process never forwards AS2
    commands again; the log says "AS2 commands disabled until the platform
    node is restarted". A later Offboard entry in the same process only holds.
    RULE: restart the AS2 stack (T3) between every flight and after any
    offboard exit or disarm.

K3  drone-xrce-agent.service (wp-g-ops) hard-codes --dev /dev/ttyTHS1. The
    link today is the FTDI cable
    (/dev/serial/by-id/usb-FTDI_TTL232R-3V3_FTCKEJ6W-if00-port0).
    Fix the unit, or rewire TELEM2 to the 40-pin UART, before Part E.

K4  bench_box.py waypoints are ABSOLUTE in the AS2 earth frame (go_to
    default frame_id 'earth', as2_python_api go_to_module.py:177), not
    relative to takeoff as the script's comment says. earth = PX4 local
    origin = where FAST-LIO started. Start perception with the vehicle on
    the takeoff mark at a fixed heading, and take off from that mark.
    earth +x = startup forward, +y = startup left.

K5  Nothing consumes /geofence/hover_request at Gate 5 (no subscriber on
    wp-b-as2). A soft breach only raises an alert. The hard action
    (NAV_LAND on /fmu/in/vehicle_command) does not depend on AS2.

K6  RC parameters in docs/px4pro_params.txt:
    RC_MAP_KILL_SW 0 (no kill switch mapped).
    COM_RC_OVERRIDE 1: stick override in auto modes only (bit 0), NOT in
    Offboard (bit 1), confirmed in PX4 v1.15.0 source (commander_params.c,
    Commander.cpp). Sticks never take the vehicle out of Offboard.
    COM_FLTMODE1/4/6 = 8/1/4: confirm in QGC which slot, if any, is POSITION.
    Covered by B2-B4.

K7  AS2 1.1.3 has no hover behaviour (motion behaviours: takeoff, go_to,
    follow_path, land). At Gate 5, "hover" means the controller holding its
    last position reference after a behaviour succeeds. That is what D1
    tests.

K8  STOP ITEM: fly only with the offboard hold patch.
    PX4 v1.15's uXRCE-DDS client publishes every /fmu/in setpoint straight
    into the controllers' uORB topics in every flight mode
    (uxrce_dds_client/dds_topics.h.em). So the unpatched platform disturbs
    POSITION flight whenever it runs armed, drops the vehicle at 15%
    throttle without attitude control when Offboard is entered before a
    behaviour starts, and fights the pilot or the watchdog's Land after an
    exit. deploy/patches/as2_platform_pixhawk_offboard_hold.patch fixes all
    three (include/as2_platform_pixhawk/offboard_guard.hpp):
      - outside Offboard: heartbeat only, and only while PX4 odometry, the
        EKF in-air flag and PX4's control mode are fresh;
      - every entry holds: in the air at the position and yaw of entry, on
        the ground with zero velocity (no takeoff);
      - AS2 commands only after a control mode set after the request or
        entry, and never again after an exit or disarm.
    gate5_as2.launch.py refuses to start without it (A0).

STOP CONDITIONS (any one: pilot takes over or kills, land, end the session,
record why)
- Pilot's call, at any time, without discussion.
- Motion in a direction other than the one commanded.
- Any change of xy/z/heading reset counters, or an EKF reset reported.
- /geofence/state not 0, or any /geofence/alert message.
- /Odometry silence or FAST-LIO warnings.
- OFFBOARD_DISABLED in the T3 log that the pilot did not cause.
- A behaviour action returning false.
- On an Offboard entry: a drop, a tilt beyond a brief correction, or drift
  over 0.20 m.
