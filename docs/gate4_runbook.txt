PASS CRITERIA (master plan)
  Position hold within +/- 10 cm for 2 continuous minutes in POSITION mode,
  zero EKF resets, no LIO degradation events in the log.

OPEN VERIFICATION DEBT TOUCHING THIS GATE
  VD-011  px4_odom_bridge EV guard never run on the live chain; the
          deliberate LiDAR power cut has never been done.
          Discharged by: Part 0, steps 0.4 and 0.6.

KNOWN RISKS (from the Gate 3 transcript)
  R1  One full EKF2 re-initialisation at rest, 2026-10-13 17:39:31,
      unexplained and unlogged. A repeat in the air breaks the pass criterion
      and drops PX4 out of POSITION. Part 0 exists to catch it on the bench.
  R2  Margin is thin. Handheld at 0.45 m/s with optical flow off, PX4 against
      LIO was 4.7 cm median, 11.7 cm max. Fly the hover gently.
  R3  No second horizontal source. Optical flow is off (EKF2_OF_CTRL 0). If
      LIO stops, EKF2 dead-reckons and PX4 falls back to Altitude within
      seconds (COM_POSCTL_NAVL 0). The pilot must be ready to fly Altitude.
  R4  Every LIO number so far is with props off. Vibration with props on is
      untested: Part 2.
  R5  People within a few metres pull the LIO estimate by decimetres (Gate 2).
      Nobody moves inside the volume during a run.

ROLES
  PILOT: RC transmitter, hand on the kill switch (RC6) for every second the
         props can turn. Sole authority to abort, no discussion.
  GROUND: terminals, calls out the bridge state and any reset.
  One person may hold both roles in Part 0 only.

ABORT TABLE (agreed before arming; pilot acts, then talks)
  Trigger                                   Pilot action
  ----------------------------------------  --------------------------------
  Any doubt                                 Land. If not instantly possible: KILL
  GROUND calls "STALE" or "BLOCKED"         Land now in POSITION if it holds,
                                            else Altitude; do not wait
  PX4 drops out of POSITION by itself       Fly Altitude, land
  GROUND calls "RESET" (any EKF reset)      Land; run is void
  Unexpected climb (terrain hold)           Throttle down in Altitude, land
  Oscillation, toilet-bowl, drift > 0.5 m   Land; if growing: KILL
  Tether snag or vehicle near the limit     KILL
  KILL: hold RC6 for 5 s or disarm. Releasing inside 5 s restarts the motors.

PART 0: QUIET BENCH RUN (props OFF, ~30 min)  [agent may run 0.2-0.6]
==========================================================================
Purpose: VD-011, look for R1 with logging on, take a fresh parameter record.

0.1 FC powered, battery or bench supply. QGC connected by USB.
    Set SDLOG_MODE 2 and SDLOG_PROFILE 129, reboot the FC, wait 5 min
    (timesync warm-up). Save the parameters from QGC to
    docs/px4_params_<date>.params: the dump in the repo is from 2026-09-13
    and predates the October changes.
    Then: python3 scripts/param_check.py docs/px4_params_<date>.params
    EXPECT "0 differ". It checks, among others: EKF2_EV_POS_Z -0.047, EKF2_OF_CTRL 0,
    EKF2_RNG_CTRL 1, EKF2_EV_CTRL 11, EKF2_HGT_REF 3, RC_MAP_KILL_SW 6,
    COM_FLTMODE1/4/6 = 8/1/2. By eye as well: EKF2_RNG_POS_* non-zero,
    GF_ACTION as decided (checklist section A).

0.2 Every terminal (bench environment; the lab WiFi breaks the normal one):
      source ~/colcon_ws/install/setup.bash
      export ROS_LOCALHOST_ONLY=1
      export CYCLONEDDS_URI=file://$HOME/colcon_ws/src/drone_bringup/config/cyclonedds_bench.xml
    T1: ros2 launch drone_bringup gate3_fusion.launch.py \
          serial_dev:=/dev/serial/by-id/usb-FTDI_TTL232RG-VSW3V3_FTADW2E6-if00-port0
    Nobody within 3 m. Wait 60 s for FAST-LIO to settle.

0.3 T2: bash ~/colcon_ws/scripts/verify_gate3.sh
    EXPECT ~10 Hz EV, one publisher, status FORWARDING with rej=0.

0.4 Leave it untouched for 15 minutes. T2:
      ros2 topic echo /px4_odom_bridge/status
    EXPECT FORWARDING throughout, fwd rising ~10 per second, rej=0.
    QGC MAVLink console at the start and the end:
      listener vehicle_local_position
    Record xy_reset_counter, z_reset_counter, heading_reset_counter.
    EXPECT all three unchanged. ANY change: this is R1. Stop here, pull the
    .ulg, do not go to Part 2 until it is explained.
    Also check the log for "PreArm:" text. It comes from the other autopilot
    on the SiK link (checklist section A) and means that item is still open.

0.5 Kill switch, props OFF: arm on RC5, kill on RC6 -> motors stop at once;
    hold -> disarms after 5 s. Arm again, kill, release at ~2 s -> motors
    restart. See it here, not in the air. Then each RC7 position in QGC:
    Stabilized / Altitude / Position.

0.6 LiDAR power cut (VD-011), disarmed, at a moment of your choosing:
    pull the LiDAR power for ~15 s, then restore it.
    EXPECT status STALE within ~1 s of the cut, then BLOCKED when data
    returns; T1 logs "EXTERNAL VISION BLOCKED" once; no further EV to PX4
    (QGC console: uorb top vehicle_visual_odometry shows 0 Hz); PX4 does
    not jump (listener vehicle_local_position stays near its last value,
    xy_valid goes false after a few seconds).
    Restart T1. EXPECT FORWARDING again after the 60 s settle.

0.7 Record: both reset-counter readings, the status lines, the .ulg name.
    PASS for Part 0: 15 min FORWARDING, zero resets, kill switch behaves,
    power cut latches BLOCKED. Anything else: no props today.

PART 2: PROPS ON, STRAPPED DOWN (vibration, R4)
==========================================================================
Vehicle strapped to a heavy base that cannot lift or slide. Tether on.
PILOT on the kill switch. Stack running as in 0.2, status FORWARDING.

2.1 Arm in Stabilized. Raise throttle slowly to just below lift, hold 30 s,
    then ~hover throttle (MPC_THR_HOVER 0.62) for 30 s if the base holds.
    Disarm.
    GROUND watches the status throughout: any STALE/BLOCKED -> KILL.
2.2 While the motors run, T2:
      ros2 topic echo /Odometry --field pose.pose.position
    EXPECT the position to stay within ~3 cm of its motors-off value. Gate 2
    measured 1.1 cm median, 2.6 cm max, motors off. If it wanders by more
    than 5 cm strapped down, LIO is being disturbed: stop, improve the
    isolation, repeat.
2.3 Pull the .ulg, open in Flight Review: vibration metrics in the green
    band, no accelerometer clipping. Reset counters unchanged.
    PASS for Part 2: green vibration, LIO within 5 cm, FORWARDING, zero
    resets. Otherwise no flight today.

==========================================================================
PART 3: TETHERED HOVER
==========================================================================
BEFORE EVERY FLIGHT
[ ] T1 freshly started with the vehicle on the take-off mark; 60 s settle.
[ ] Status FORWARDING, rej=0. Reset counters recorded as the baseline.
[ ] T3: bag of /Odometry /fmu/out/vehicle_odometry /px4_odom_bridge/status
        (never /livox/lidar or /cloud_registered in flight):
        ros2 bag record -o ~/flight_logs/gate4_<date>_<n> /Odometry \
          /fmu/out/vehicle_odometry /fmu/out/vehicle_local_position \
          /px4_odom_bridge/status /px4_odom_bridge/state
[ ] Nobody inside the volume. Abort table read aloud once.

3.1 Hop: arm in Altitude, lift to ~0.3 m, 5 s, land. Checks trim, motor
    order in the air, and that Altitude is flyable (the R3 fallback).
3.2 Altitude hover at ~1.0 m for 30 s. Land.
3.3 Take off in Altitude to ~1.0 m, sticks centred, switch RC7 to POSITION.
    EXPECT the vehicle holds. A lurch on the switch: back to Altitude, land,
    stop (frame or estimator problem).
3.4 POSITION hover, hands off the sticks as far as it is safe, for at least
    150 s (the criterion needs 120 s continuous). GROUND calls the status
    every 30 s. Land in POSITION. Disarm.
3.5 Repeat 3.3-3.4 until two runs meet the criterion, battery permitting.
    Land at the first low-battery warning.
