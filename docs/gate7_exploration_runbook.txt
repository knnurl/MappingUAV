WHAT THE EXPLORER DOES
  It picks the next viewpoint that shows the most unknown space at ONE
  altitude (z_fly), sends it to the planner, and repeats until nothing
  unknown is reachable. It never arms, takes off, lands or changes mode: the
  pilot or Aerostack2 does. At the end it HOLDS (on_done: hold) and waits to
  be landed. It stops by itself (PAUSED, no goals) when: the pilot takes over,
  LIO degrades, the watchdog or the odometry bridge reports trouble, the map
  stops answering, or the estimate and LIO disagree. Low battery ends the
  mission and flies back to the start point.

HAZARDS SPECIFIC TO EXPLORATION
  H1  It goes where it has never been. Walk the whole fenced volume first:
      no glass, no mirrors, no hanging cables, no thin poles (anything thinner
      than ~5 cm may not be in a 0.2 m map), doors fixed open or shut.
  H2  Nobody inside the volume. A person both blocks paths and pulls the LIO
      estimate (Gate 2). Spotters stand outside the geofence.
  H3  It will fly out of the pilot's direct line of sight if the volume has
      more than one room. First flights: ONE room, door shut (Part C).
  H4  Single altitude. It does not look under tables or over cabinets, and it
      treats space it has not seen as not flyable.
  H5  Battery. Plan each run to end above 40%. The interlock is at 25%.
  H6  After any pause caused by a takeover, restart T3 (Aerostack2) before
      "resume" (the platform forwards nothing after an Offboard exit).

ABORT: Gate 5/6 stop conditions, plus
  - a goal outside what you believe is free space;
  - two goals in a row that fail ("goal dropped");
  - the vehicle out of sight for more than a few seconds (multi-room runs
    need a second pilot-side observer with a live view).
  Pilot action: ros2 service call /frontier_explorer/pause is NOT the abort.
  The abort is the RC: POSITION, or KILL.

PART C: ONE ROOM, TETHERED, PROPS ON
==========================================================================
BEFORE EVERY FLIGHT: Gate 5 list; stack freshly started on the mark; 60 s
settle; preflight all PASS; flight_log_session.sh running; battery > 80%.
Take off with the Gate 5 takeoff action to z_fly. Wait 10 s.

C1  First mission, door shut, max_mission_s 300:
      ros2 service call /frontier_explorer/start std_srvs/srv/Trigger
    EXPECT WAIT_MAP -> SELECTING -> NAVIGATING; each goal visible in
    /frontier_explorer/status BEFORE the vehicle moves; goals only inside the
    room; at the end "DONE: COMPLETE" and the vehicle holding.
    Land with the Gate 5 land action.
    A room fully visible from the start gives DONE with no goal at all:
    that is correct. Put one large box in the room to create a frontier.
C2  Pause and resume in flight: during NAVIGATING
      ros2 service call /frontier_explorer/pause std_srvs/srv/Trigger
    EXPECT the vehicle stops and holds; state PAUSED: OPERATOR. Then
    .../resume. EXPECT it continues with a new goal.
C3  Takeover: during NAVIGATING the pilot flips to POSITION.
    EXPECT explorer PAUSED: I2, no further goals, the vehicle holding in
    POSITION. Land. (H6: restart before the next run.)
C4  Stop: during NAVIGATING .../stop. EXPECT IDLE and a hold.
C5  Blocked goal: after DONE in C1, repeat with a box moved to where the
    first goal was. EXPECT a different goal or "goal dropped" then another
    goal; never contact.
C6  Low battery (only if C1-C5 were clean): set battery_min_fraction to a
    value the battery will cross within the flight (for example current
    reading minus 5 points). EXPECT "DONE: BATTERY" and the vehicle flying
    back to the take-off point and holding. Land. Restore 0.25.
